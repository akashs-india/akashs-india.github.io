I'm Akash, a software engineer. I build large distributed systems and spend most of my time on
three things: making them **scale**, keeping them **secure**, and figuring out how **AI** changes
the way we build them.

**Now.** I'm a senior software engineer at Microsoft, working on Microsoft 365 services that handle
trillions of requests a day. I'm also an AI champion in M365, which means building AI-native tools and
helping engineering teams change how they work.

**Before.** I spent several years on Exchange, the backend behind Microsoft's mail and calendar services.
I shipped modern authentication and identity work, led the response to critical zero-day
vulnerabilities, and kept mail flowing through large cloud migrations. Earlier, I built anomaly
detection and reinforcement-learning systems at Siemens R&D and contributed to the Linux Foundation's
OpenPrinting project through Google Summer of Code.

**Education.** I have a master's in computer science from Georgia Tech and a B.Tech in computer science
from IIT Mandi, where I received the President of India Gold Medal.

This site is where I write about that work in depth: architecture, failure stories, security, and
what it takes to build real software with AI.
